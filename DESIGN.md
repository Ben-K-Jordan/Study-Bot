# Design system: Study Bot

## Overview

North star: a Google app that turns into a lesson when you start studying.

Study Bot has two visual modes. The shell covers Home, Plan, Study, Cards, Guides, Chat, Progress, Settings and the sign-in pages. It follows Google Material 3 the way Google's own web apps use it: cool white surfaces, pale blue-grey tonal containers, one blue for actions, pill-shaped buttons, 16px cards, and a navigation rail with pill indicators. The aim is the category standard done carefully, so the shell never competes with what it shows, which is mostly exam countdowns and the things the student still gets wrong.

A study session or a flashcard review is the second mode. It takes the whole screen, the rail and app bar slide away, and the screen follows the grammar of a Duolingo lesson: one question at a time, a thick progress bar, answer cards with a 2px border and a 4px bottom lip that presses down, and a feedback bar that slides up after each answer. The colours switch to a separate lesson palette drawn from Google's product blue, green and red. The type changes too. Google Sans Flex has a roundness axis (ROND), and the session scope turns it from 0 to 100 while the weight steps up. This document calls that the roundness dial. It is the one moment of motion the system builds around.

Both modes come in a light and a dark theme that follow the system setting, with a manual switch in the top bar, Settings and the More sheet. The old dark slate dashboard of stat tiles and the later chalkboard theme are retired and are not references. There are no mascots, confetti, cartoon badges, emoji, or game currency like hearts and gems. XP and streaks exist, but they sit in one line of small text.

Motion is short and only reports state. State layers, colour changes and presses take 150ms, progress and sheets 250ms, the feedback sheet and the returning shell 350ms, and the roundness dial 600ms. State changes use the M3 standard curve, entrances the emphasized curve, and exits the emphasized accelerate curve. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` every animation and transition drops to 0.01ms and view transitions to zero, and the type is simply rounded on arrival.

In development, `/design` renders every token, component and lesson primitive in both themes, with contrast measured live. It returns 404 in production.

What holds across both modes:

- Depth comes from tonal surfaces and hairlines. Shadows appear only on things that float.
- Each group of actions has at most one filled button.
- Type comes from a fixed M3 scale in Google Sans Flex. Nothing is fluid.
- Lesson controls feel physical: 2px borders, a 4px lip, a 2px press.
- A state is never shown by colour alone. There is always a word, a number or an icon.
- All motion stops under reduced motion.

## Colors

The shell uses Material 3 colour roles at Google's web baseline values, and sessions add their own palette of Google product colours.

Every colour is a custom property in `src/app/globals.css`, with light values on `:root` and dark values on `.dark` (next-themes sets the class). Components reach them through semantic Tailwind classes such as `bg-surface-container` and `text-on-surface-variant`, so a component never needs a `dark:` override. In the frontmatter, keys ending in `-dark` are the `.dark` values. The shadcn names map onto the roles: background is surface, card is surface container lowest, popover is surface container low (high in dark), muted is surface container, accent is surface container high, border is outline variant, input is outline, and ring is primary.

### Primary

- Primary blue, `primary` (#0b57d0, #a8c7fa in dark): filled buttons, text and outlined button labels, links, the selected tab indicator, the focus ring, the text caret and the first chart series.
- Primary container, `primary-container` (#d3e3fd, #0842a0 in dark): the featured countdown card for the nearest exam, info alerts, and the Start session button in the rail and on phones.

### Secondary

- Secondary container, `secondary-container` (#c2e7ff, #004a77 in dark): the active navigation indicator, tonal buttons, the selected segment in segmented buttons, text selection, and the track of the linear progress indicator.

### Tertiary

- Success green, `tertiary` (#146c2e, #6dd58c in dark): success and high mastery. Its container (#c4eed0, #0f5223 in dark) backs success badges and success alerts.

### Neutral

- Surface (#f8fafd, #131314 in dark): the page, the top app bar and the rail. It is also the browser theme colour.
- Surface container lowest (#ffffff, #0e0e0e in dark): cards and the sign-in card.
- Surface container (#f0f4f9, #1e1f20 in dark): the bottom navigation bar, default alerts, the sign-in page behind its card, and neutral feedback in sessions.
- Surface container high (#e9eef6, #282a2c in dark): dialogs, hover accents and the student's chat bubbles.
- Surface container highest (#dde3ea, #333537 in dark): the mastery bar track and the off state of switches.
- On surface (#1f1f1f, #e3e3e3 in dark) for primary text, and on surface variant (#444746, #c4c7c5 in dark) for secondary text, icons and inactive navigation.
- Outline (#747775, #8e918f in dark): text field borders and outlined buttons. Outline variant (#c4c7c5, #444746 in dark): card hairlines, dividers and the scrollbar thumb.
- Inverse surface (#303030, #e3e3e3 in dark) for tooltips and snackbars, with inverse primary for snackbar actions.
- Scrim, black at 32% behind dialogs and sheets.

### Status

- Error red, `error` (#b3261e, #f2b8b5 in dark), and its container (#f9dedc, #8c1d18 in dark): destructive buttons, invalid fields and failure alerts.
- Warning amber, `warning` (#995c00, #fdd663 in dark), and its container (#feefc3, #5a4300 in dark): missed sessions, due states, low mastery and the "1 missed" chip.

### Charts

Five series roles: blue, green, amber, red and purple (`chart-1` to `chart-5`), with lighter steps in dark.

### Lesson palette

Inside a session the surface is plain white (#ffffff, #131314 in dark), and controls take the `lesson-*` tokens.

- Neutral options: white fill, #dadce0 border and lip, #f8f9fa on hover, with #3c4043 for neutral button labels and #5f6368 for muted text such as the question counter.
- Selected: #e8f0fe fill, #1a73e8 border, #185abc lip and #174ea6 text.
- Primary lesson button (#1a73e8, #1967d2 on hover, #185abc lip): Start session, Resume session, and Continue after neutral feedback.
- Check green (#188038, #137333 on hover, #0d652d lip): the Check button and Continue after a correct answer. A correct answer card and the correct feedback sheet use #e6f4ea with #137333 text.
- Wrong red (#c5221f, #b31412 on hover, #a50e0e lip): Continue after a wrong answer. A wrong card and the wrong feedback sheet use #fce8e6 with #a50e0e text.
- Disabled: #e8eaed with #80868b text.
- Progress: a #e8eaed track and a #1e8e3e fill.

Hover fills step away from the label colour. They get darker under white labels in light and lighter under dark labels in dark, so contrast goes up on hover. Sessions use blue, green and red; the warning amber stays in the shell.

### Named rules

**The lesson scope rule.** `lesson-*` tokens appear only inside `.lesson`, which wraps the `/s/[sessionId]` layout and the flashcard review layout. Menus, dialogs and toasts that open during a session portal out to `<body>` and keep the shell colours.

**The words first rule.** Colour never carries a state alone. Badges print their word, mastery bars print the percentage, and answer cards add a check or a cross. In forced colours mode, selected and current states switch to a Highlight border or fill so they stay visible.

## Typography

Google Sans Flex sets everything. It loads as a variable font from `@fontsource-variable/google-sans-flex` with the ROND axis file and falls back to Google Sans, then the system UI stack. Google Sans Code sets inline code and preformatted text. The key hint chips stay in Google Sans Flex at 11px. The heading font token points at the same family, so headings differ only by size and weight. One family reads like a Google product in the shell and turns softer and friendlier in sessions, without a second typeface.

Sizes come from fifteen `type-*` utilities that copy the M3 type scale in fixed rem. There is no fluid type. A few headings step up one size at 600px instead; the lesson question goes from headline-s to headline-m. Body text defaults to body-m. Times, counts and scores use tabular figures.

| headline-l | 32 / 40px | 400 | 0 | The end screen heading from 600px, status words in countdown cards |
| headline-m | 28 / 36px | 400 | 0 | The lesson question from 600px (700 in sessions), session screen headings |
| headline-s | 24 / 32px | 400 | 0 | View headlines, dialog titles, the feedback sheet title, the lesson question on phones |
| title-l | 22 / 28px | 400 | 0 | The top app bar title, sheet titles, settings sections |

Running text sits at body-l with a measure of 65 to 75 characters (`max-w-prose`).

### The roundness dial

The shell sets `font-variation-settings: "ROND" 0` on `html`, with weights 400 and 500. The `.lesson` scope sets ROND 100 and a base weight of 500. Questions, headings, lesson buttons and confidence chips are 700, and answer text is 500. On entry the change animates through `@starting-style`: 600ms on `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` after a 120ms delay, so the first frame paints square and the turn is visible. While the session's loading skeleton is on screen the dial waits at 0, which means the student sees the real question's glyphs round. Moving between session screens afterwards keeps it at 100. Portaled menus, tooltips, dialogs and toasts get ROND 100 through a rule on `body:has(.lesson)`.

### Named rules

**The scale rule.** Use the `type-*` utilities for text size, never Tailwind's `text-*` sizes. The `text-*` sizes share a prefix with the `text-*` colour classes, and `cn()` can drop one of the two when both appear on an element. The `type-*` utilities don't collide.

## Layout

The shell follows M3 window size classes, set as the Tailwind breakpoints in place of Tailwind's defaults: compact under 600px, medium from 600px (`sm`), expanded from 840px (`md`), large from 1200px (`lg`) and extra large from 1600px (`xl`).

Under 600px a fixed bottom navigation bar, 80px tall, holds Home, Plan, Study and Cards. More opens a bottom sheet with Guides, Chat, Progress, Settings and the theme switch. From 600px a fixed navigation rail, 80px wide, holds the app mark, the Start session button when a session is due today, the seven destinations, and Settings at the bottom. A fixed top app bar, 64px tall, carries the page title as the page's only h1, the theme toggle and the account menu. Its title lines up with the content column.

Content sits in `PageContainer`. Gutters are 16px, 24px from 600px and 32px from 840px. Sections sit 24px apart, and the page ends with 40px of space. There are three widths: narrow (48rem) for forms, chat and guides, default (72rem), and wide (80rem) for the plan grid. All three start at the same left edge, where the wide column starts, and only the right edge moves, so shared controls like the course switcher stay in place between destinations.

Spacing runs on Tailwind's 4px step. Inside a section, items usually sit 12px apart, and cards pad 24px (16px in the small size). On phones the exam countdown cards scroll sideways with snap points, 12px apart. From 600px they form a grid of two columns with 16px gaps, and four columns from 1200px.

Short windows (at most 32rem tall, which covers a phone in landscape and a laptop at 200 to 400% zoom) are their own variant, `short:`. Sticky and fixed bars go static there so they never cover the focused control, the floating Start session button hides, and the shell metrics that clear the bottom bar drop to zero. Everywhere else, focused elements scroll clear of the bars through scroll margins built from `--app-bar-height` and `--bottom-bar-height`. On phones the floating Start session button sits 16px above the bottom bar and the page leaves room for it. In grids, a child that holds truncating text or a scroller gets `min-w-0`, or it overflows at 390px.

Entering a session or a card review, the shell recedes through React view transitions. The rail slides out to the left, the app bar up and the bottom bar down, over 250ms on the emphasized accelerate curve. Coming back, they slide in over 350ms on the emphasized curve.

A session has no rail or app bar. `LessonFrame` puts a sticky top bar (close button, progress bar, question counter and phase) in a 56rem column, the question and answer area in a 42rem column with 16px gutters (32px from 600px), and a sticky footer for the actions, which the feedback sheet replaces after an answer. The frame publishes the footer's height as `--lesson-footer-h`, so a field scrolled into view stops above it. On phones the footer stacks Skip above Check, which puts the main action nearest the thumb; from 600px they sit side by side.

The sign-in and account pages show one card on a surface container page, up to 30rem wide from 600px and 65rem with two columns from 840px. Under 600px the page itself is the card.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tonal first. Surfaces separate by stepping through the surface container ramp, and a resting card gets a 1px outline variant ring instead of a shadow. A shadow means something is hovered, open or floating. The shadows are the M3 elevation levels, each a tight key shadow plus a softer ambient one.

### Shadow vocabulary

- Flat, `shadow-xs` (`0 0 #0000`): inputs and resting cards.
- Level 1, `shadow-sm` (`0 1px 2px 0 rgb(0 0 0 / 0.3), 0 1px 3px 1px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.15)`): a hovered countdown card, hovered filled and tonal buttons, and the rail's Start session button on hover.
- Level 2, `shadow-md` (`0 1px 2px 0 rgb(0 0 0 / 0.3), 0 2px 6px 2px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.15)`): menus, popovers, select lists and the floating Start session button on phones.
- Level 3, `shadow-lg` (`0 1px 3px 0 rgb(0 0 0 / 0.3), 0 4px 8px 3px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.15)`): dialogs, sheets, the snackbar, and the floating button on hover.
- `shadow-xl` and `shadow-2xl` are defined at levels 4 and 5. Only the chart tooltip uses `shadow-xl`.

Dialogs and sheets also sit over the scrim at 32%.

Sessions add one depth device of their own, the lip. Every answer card, confidence chip and lesson button carries a solid `box-shadow: 0 4px 0` in a darker shade of its own colour, set through `--lip`. Pressing moves the control down 2px and shrinks the lip to 2px, like a key going down, over 150ms. Disabled and busy controls don't press.

### Named rules

**The tonal first rule.** Nothing at rest has a shadow. A shadow appears only when something is hovered, open or floating, and the lesson lip is the one exception because it is the press affordance.

## Shapes

Corners follow the M3 shape scale, pinned to the Tailwind radius steps the components use.

- Pills (`rounded-full`): every shell button, badges, chips, navigation indicators, switches, progress bars and the scrollbar thumb.
- 4px (`rounded-sm`): tooltips, the snackbar, key hints and inline code.
- 8px (`rounded-md`): text fields, textareas, select triggers and menus.
- 12px (`rounded-lg`): alerts, list rows, and the key hint marker inside an answer card.
- 16px (`rounded-2xl`): cards, countdown cards, the focus shape of navigation destinations, and every lesson control (buttons, answer cards, confidence chips, the recall box). The Start session button is a 56px square at 16px, the M3 FAB shape.
- 24px (`rounded-3xl`): chat bubbles, with the corner on the student's side cut to 8px.
- 28px (`rounded-4xl`): dialogs, the top corners of bottom sheets, and the sign-in card. Side sheets round only their inner corners, at 16px.

The shell draws edges with 1px lines: an outline variant ring around cards and dividers, and an outline border on text fields and outlined buttons. Lesson controls use 2px borders, and the session footer has a 2px top border.

Focus is a 2px solid outline in the ring colour at a 2px offset on every button, link, tab and other action. Inside a clipping container a component sets `--focus-ring-offset: -2px` to draw it inside. Answer cards, confidence chips and lesson buttons draw it 6px outside the control, around the lip. Text fields don't take the outline; their border turns primary and thickens to 2px instead.

## Components

Components live in `src/components/ui` (shadcn/ui on Radix, restyled to M3), `src/components/app` (app-level pieces), `src/components/shell` (the frame) and `src/components/lesson` (session primitives).

### Buttons

Shell buttons are M3 pills with label-l text. The default is 40px tall with a 48px touch target drawn by an invisible `::after`; there are 28, 32 and 48px sizes, and icon buttons at the same sizes. Filled (primary) is the one main action in a group. Tonal (secondary container) is the softer alternative, outlined (a 1px outline border with primary text) sits beside a filled button, text (primary text) is for dialog actions and links like "All sessions", ghost is neutral for icon buttons and toolbars, and destructive is filled error for confirming a delete.

Hover, focus and press come from the state layer, an overlay in the content colour at 8% on hover and 10% on focus and press, fading over 150ms on the standard curve. Filled, tonal and destructive buttons also take `shadow-sm` on hover. Disabled buttons drop to 38% text on a 12% fill. A disabled button that holds a spinner is busy, so it keeps its colours and shows an in-progress label that reuses the verb, such as "Saving…".

### Chips and badges

Badges are 24px pills with label-m text in one tonal container per meaning: default (primary), secondary, success, warning, destructive (error container), outline and ghost. The text carries the meaning. The "AI-written" tag that sits beside every block of AI-written text is an outline badge with a sparkle icon; inside a session it takes the colour of the text around it.

Segmented choices, such as the theme switch in Settings, use the toggle group in its outline variant with no spacing. The selected segment fills with secondary container and shows a check.

### Cards and containers

Cards are surface container lowest with a 1px outline variant ring, 16px corners, 24px padding (16px in the small size) and title-m titles. An image at the top or bottom takes the card's corners.

The countdown card is one exam: the days left in display-m, the date, plan progress on a linear indicator, and a "1 missed" warning badge with Reschedule. The nearest exam is the featured variant, in primary container with no ring and 20px padding. The whole card is the link to its plan, so the card takes `shadow-sm` on hover and draws the focus ring itself.

Alerts are 12px containers with 12px by 16px padding in five tones: default (surface container), info, success, warning and destructive. Only destructive announces itself as an alert by default.

### Inputs and fields

Text fields are outlined: 48px tall, 8px corners, a 1px outline border, a transparent fill, and 16px text with 16px side padding. Hover darkens the border to on surface. Focus turns it primary and 2px wide. An invalid field turns the border error and shows its message beside the field. Disabled fields drop to 38% opacity. Textareas grow with their content from 96px. Labels always sit above the field, and placeholders are realistic examples or nothing.

Switches are the M3 52 by 32px switch. The thumb grows from 16px to 24px when it turns on, and the track fills with primary.

### Navigation

Each destination is a 24px icon in a 56 by 32px pill indicator, with a label-m label under it. The current destination fills its indicator with secondary container and draws its icon with a heavier stroke (2.25 against 1.75). Hover and focus lay the state layer on the indicator. The rail is surface, the bottom bar is surface container, and the More sheet opens from the bottom bar with the remaining destinations in a grid of four.

Tabs are M3 primary tabs: title-s labels, 48px tall, inactive labels in on surface variant, and a 3px primary indicator with rounded top corners under the active one. The `line` variant is the M3 secondary tab with a 2px square indicator.

### Dialogs, sheets, tooltips and toasts

Dialogs are surface container high with 28px corners, 24px padding, headline-s titles and `shadow-lg`. They fade and zoom in from 95% over 200ms on the emphasized curve. The cancel action is a text button, and the confirming action is filled, or destructive for a delete. Bottom sheets are surface container low with 28px top corners and scroll inside a cap of 85% of the viewport height.

Tooltips are the M3 plain tooltip: inverse surface, 4px corners and body-s text, after a 300ms delay. Toasts are M3 snackbars at the bottom centre, above the bottom bar: inverse surface, 4px corners, `shadow-lg`, actions in inverse primary, and a 32px close button inside the trailing edge.

### Progress and stats

The M3 linear indicator (`ProgressLinear`) is 4px tall: an active bar, a 4px gap, the rest of the track in secondary container, and a stop dot at the end. Without a value it sweeps as an indeterminate bar, which stops under reduced motion. `MasteryBar` is an 8px meter on surface container highest with its percentage always printed and its fill in warning, primary or success by level. `StatLine` is the stats row on Home: one line of body-m text in on surface variant, with values in on surface and decorative dot separators.

### Icons

Icons come from lucide-react only, usually at 16, 20 or 24px. Most keep lucide's default 2px stroke. Navigation icons use 1.75 (2.25 for the current destination), and the check and cross marks in sessions use 3. Icons are `aria-hidden` and sit beside a text label, and icon-only buttons are named for their action.

### Lesson primitives

These are the signature components, in `src/components/lesson`. The screen handles the keyboard (1 to 4 for options, Q, W and E for confidence, Enter to check and continue); the primitives only draw the key hints, and only on fine pointers.

`LessonFrame` is the full-screen frame described under Layout. Its close button is a 44px circle with an X, and the counter beside the progress bar reads "Question 3 of 12" in label-l at 700, followed by the phase in exam simulation. The question is the page's h1, the largest thing on screen. The frame also holds the polite live region that announces each result.

`LessonProgress` is the thick bar: 16px tall and fully rounded, with a grey track, a green fill that eases its width over 350ms on the emphasized curve, and a 5px white highlight at 30% along the top of the fill.

`LessonButton` is at least 48px tall and 128px wide, with 16px corners, a 2px border and the lip, and 16px text at 700 with 0.2px tracking. Its variants are check (green, submits an answer), correct (green, continues), wrong (red, continues), primary (blue, starts or resumes a session) and neutral (white with a grey border, for Skip and secondary actions). Disabled is flat grey. Loading keeps the colours and shows a spinner beside its loading label. An Enter key hint sits inside on fine pointers.

`AnswerOption` is a real radio input inside a card at least 64px tall, with 16px corners, a 2px border, the lip, and 18/28px text at 500. A 32px marker at the start shows the key hint on fine pointers and a radio dot on touch screens. Selecting a card turns it blue and fills the marker. After checking, the right answer turns green with a check and a wrong pick turns red with a cross, and the other cards dim to 60%.

`ConfidencePicker` is three equal chips, Guessing, Unsure and Sure, built like answer options at 48px tall with a check on the chosen one.

`FreeRecallInput` is the answer box: a 2px border, 16px corners and 18/28px text at 500. It grows from 112px (128px from 840px), and focus turns its border selected blue.

`FeedbackSheet` replaces the footer after an answer and slides up from the bottom over 350ms on the emphasized curve. Correct is pale green with a green check badge, wrong is pale red with a red cross badge, and neutral (a pretest, or a model answer to score against) uses the shell's surface container with an info badge. The title is headline-s at 700 with a short note under it. Continue is the one action, in the tone's button colour, and it takes focus so Enter moves on. The sheet caps at 60% of the viewport height (35% from 840px) and scrolls. Longer feedback reads in the page under the answered question, which stays on screen.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Do use the semantic role classes (`bg-surface-container`, `text-on-surface-variant`, `border-outline-variant`) and let the theme switch them.
- Do keep one filled button per group, and make the others tonal, outlined or text.
- Do set every text size with a `type-*` utility, and use `tabular-nums` for times, counts and scores.
- Do give every pressable lesson control the lip (`lesson-lip` with `--lip` set to a darker shade of its colour).
- Do save shadows for hovered, open and floating things (`shadow-sm`, `shadow-md`, `shadow-lg`).
- Do pair every status colour with a word, a printed number or an icon.
- Do keep the focus ring visible: 2px at a 2px offset, -2px inside clipping containers, 6px around lesson cards.
- Do check text pairs at 4.5:1 and other UI parts at 3:1 in both themes on `/design`.

### Don't

- Don't use raw palette classes such as `bg-blue-500`, or `dark:` colour overrides.
- Don't use `lesson-*` tokens outside `.lesson`.
- Don't use Tailwind's `text-*` sizes or `clamp()` for UI text.
- Don't put a shadow on a resting card or an input.
- Don't add mascots, confetti, cartoon badges, emoji, or game currency such as hearts, gems or coins.
- Don't show a running score or the navigation during a session. The close button is the only way out.
- Don't type check marks, crosses or arrows as text icons. Use a lucide icon with `aria-hidden` beside a text label.
- Don't bring back the dark slate dashboard of stat tiles or the chalkboard theme.
- Don't ship motion that keeps running under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
